Claude Opus 5.5

Her day-to-day favorite despite a low blind grade. She liked it as an orchestrator in Claude Projects and preferred its design work when it had an existing design system to follow. Her strongest OpenAI result for coding and a model she credited with genuinely novel pixel-level work on the interactive storybook. The judges gave it the highest usable-as-is score. She found it frustrating in Codex day to day and less compelling to reach for given the alternatives. She left open how much of that came from the model and how much came from its harness. 06:49 · 10:44 · 19:09 · 27:10

GPT-6.2 Sol

Her writing leader, with concise, clear output. She considered it cheap and useful for browser work. It had the highest annoyance rate, driven by work handed back to her, frustration, and repeated requests to continue. She cited having to push it to open a pull request. 11:10 · 27:00 · 28:21

Claude Fable 5.2

Her overall leader before Neon entered the benchmark and still a model she liked for video editing, animation, and substantial coding work. She found its front-end and SVG design sense weaker. It was the slow, high-cost choice she reserved for tasks where its strengths mattered. 08:04 · 16:51 · 23:21 · 26:32

Gemini 4 Carbon

It finished last overall and the presenter said it worked poorly in her workflow. The scorecards still show strength on specific tasks. It tied for first on the graph runtime audit and assistant voice and led the Slack reply, while its app category score was 1.6. 09:06 · 13:23
